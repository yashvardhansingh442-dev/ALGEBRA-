# Solutions — Factoring

Matches problem numbers in `practice-problems.md`.

## Easy

**S1.** `4x + 12`
- GCF of 4 and 12 is `4`
- **Answer: 4(x + 3)**

**S2.** `5x² - 10x`
- GCF is `5x`
- **Answer: 5x(x - 2)**

**S3.** `x² + 5x + 6`
- Two numbers multiplying to `6`, adding to `5`: `2` and `3`
- **Answer: (x + 2)(x + 3)**

**S4.** `x² - 9`
- Difference of squares: `x² - 3²`
- **Answer: (x - 3)(x + 3)**

**S5.** `x² + 8x + 15`
- Multiply to `15`, add to `8`: `3` and `5`
- **Answer: (x + 3)(x + 5)**

## Medium

**S6.** `x² - x - 12`
- Multiply to `-12`, add to `-1`: `-4` and `3`
- **Answer: (x - 4)(x + 3)**

**S7.** `x² - 10x + 21`
- Multiply to `21`, add to `-10`: both negative → `-3` and `-7`
- **Answer: (x - 3)(x - 7)**

**S8.** `3x³ - 6x² + 9x`
- GCF is `3x`; dividing each term gives `x²`, `-2x`, `3`
- The remaining trinomial `x² - 2x + 3` needs two numbers multiplying to `3` and adding to `-2` — none exist, so it doesn't factor further
- **Answer: 3x(x² - 2x + 3)**

**S9.** `x² - 64`
- Difference of squares: `x² - 8²`
- **Answer: (x - 8)(x + 8)**

**S10.** `x² + 10x + 25`
- Perfect square trinomial: `x²`, `5² = 25`, and the middle `10x = 2·x·5` ✓
- **Answer: (x + 5)²**

## Hard

**S11.** `2x² + 5x + 3`
- `a · c = 2 · 3 = 6`; need two numbers multiplying to `6` and adding to `5`: `2` and `3`
- Split the middle: `2x² + 2x + 3x + 3`
- Group: `2x(x + 1) + 3(x + 1)`
- **Answer: (2x + 3)(x + 1)**

**S12.** `3x² - 10x + 8`
- `a · c = 3 · 8 = 24`; need two numbers multiplying to `24` and adding to `-10`: `-4` and `-6`
- Split the middle: `3x² - 4x - 6x + 8`
- Group: `x(3x - 4) - 2(3x - 4)`
- **Answer: (3x - 4)(x - 2)**

**S13.** `2x² - 18`
- GCF first: `2(x² - 9)`
- `x² - 9` is a difference of squares: `(x - 3)(x + 3)`
- **Answer: 2(x - 3)(x + 3)**

**S14.** `x² - 14x + 49`
- Perfect square trinomial: `x²`, `7² = 49`, middle `14x = 2·x·7` with a negative sign
- **Answer: (x - 7)²**

**S15.** `6x² + x - 2`
- `a · c = 6 · (-2) = -12`; need two numbers multiplying to `-12` and adding to `1`: `4` and `-3`
- Split the middle: `6x² + 4x - 3x - 2`
- Group: `2x(3x + 2) - 1(3x + 2)`
- **Answer: (3x + 2)(2x - 1)**
