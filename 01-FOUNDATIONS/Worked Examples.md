# Worked Examples — Foundations

Each example shows every step. Try covering the solution and working it out yourself first.

---

### Example 1: Integer arithmetic
**Problem:** Evaluate `-8 + 3 - (-5)`

**Step 1:** Rewrite subtracting a negative as adding a positive: `-8 + 3 + 5`
**Step 2:** Add left to right: `-8 + 3 = -5`
**Step 3:** `-5 + 5 = 0`

**Answer: 0**

---

### Example 2: Order of operations
**Problem:** Evaluate `6 + (2 + 3)² ÷ 5`

**Step 1:** Parentheses first: `(2 + 3) = 5` → `6 + 5² ÷ 5`
**Step 2:** Exponent: `5² = 25` → `6 + 25 ÷ 5`
**Step 3:** Division: `25 ÷ 5 = 5` → `6 + 5`
**Step 4:** Addition: `6 + 5 = 11`

**Answer: 11**

---

### Example 3: Order of operations with multiple operations at the same tier
**Problem:** Evaluate `20 ÷ 4 × 5`

This is a common trap — people assume multiplication happens before division because of how PEMDAS is often taught. It doesn't. Division and multiplication are equal priority — go left to right.

**Step 1:** `20 ÷ 4 = 5` (leftmost operation first)
**Step 2:** `5 × 5 = 25`

**Answer: 25** (not 1 — that would be wrong)

### Example 4: Isolating a variable
**Problem:** Solve for x: `3x - 4 = 11`

**Step 1:** Add 4 to both sides: `3x - 4 + 4 = 11 + 4` → `3x = 15`
**Step 2:** Divide both sides by 3: `3x ÷ 3 = 15 ÷ 3` → `x = 5`

**Check:** Substitute back in: `3(5) - 4 = 15 - 4 = 11` ✓

**Answer: x = 5**

---

### Example 5: Combining integers and variables
**Problem:** Solve for x: `-2x + 7 = -3`

**Step 1:** Subtract 7 from both sides: `-2x = -3 - 7` → `-2x = -10`
**Step 2:** Divide both sides by -2 (dividing by a negative keeps the equation balanced, sign included): `x = -10 ÷ -2` → `x = 5`

**Check:** `-2(5) + 7 = -10 + 7 = -3` ✓

**Answer: x = 5**
